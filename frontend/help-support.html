Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common TaskFlow questions.

You can create a task from multiple screens in TaskFlow:

  • Navigate to My Tasks or any project board.
  • Click the primary blue + New Task button in the top action bar, or click + Add Task directly within any Kanban column (Backlog, In Progress, Review, or Done).
  • Fill in the task title, description, priority badge, and deadline, then click Create Task.

To create a new collaborative project:

  • Click Projects in the left sidebar, then select + New Project, or navigate directly to the Create Project page.
  • Enter your project name, key objective, department tag, and target completion deadline.
  • Select a project lead, choose team collaborators, and select a color swatch. Click Create Project to generate the workspace board immediately.

Open any task card to launch the Task Details screen. In the right-hand inspection panel under Assignee, click the member dropdown or avatar slot. Select any collaborator from your project team. The assigned teammate will instantly receive an in-app notification and email alert based on their notification preferences.

Click the Notification Bell in the top header (with the blue unread indicator dot), or navigate directly to the Notifications page. Here you can filter by All, Mentions, Assigned, and System Alerts, or use Mark All as Read to clear pending badges.

Click your user avatar in the top header or bottom sidebar and select My Profile. Click the Edit Profile button in the page header to enable input mode. You can update your display name, contact phone, job title, location, and bio, or hover over your profile picture to upload a new avatar photo. Click Save Changes to store your updates.

Navigate to Settings from the left sidebar and select the Account & Security tab. Enter your current password, followed by your new password and confirmation. The real-time password checklist will verify at least 8 characters, an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character. Click Change Password to finalize.

Open Settings and click the Language & Region category. Choose your preferred interface language (including English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Spanish, French, or German), configure your date and 12/24-hour time format, and click Save Regional Settings.

If form updates or profile changes do not persist:

  • Check for red validation hints beneath required fields (e.g. required full name or matching passwords).
  • Ensure you have clicked the blue Save Changes button rather than navigating away immediately.
  • Check that your browser allows local storage and cookies for the application domain.
  • If issues persist, use the Contact Support button below to submit a technical support request.
